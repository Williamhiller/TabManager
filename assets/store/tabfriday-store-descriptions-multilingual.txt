TabFriday - Auto Group Tabs with Custom Rules

Too many tabs open at once? TabFriday automatically turns messy Chrome windows into clean, organized tab groups.

Too many tabs? Auto-group them by site, topic, or rules. Remove duplicates, mute, sleep, and auto-close idle tabs. Free forever.

Why install TabFriday?

1. Auto-group tabs by rules
Group tabs by website, topic, domain, or your own custom rules. Set up rules once, and TabFriday keeps sorting new tabs for you.

2. Remove duplicate tabs automatically
Clean repeated pages without hunting through every window.

3. Keep Chrome lighter
Mute noisy pages, sleep idle tabs, auto-close tabs you no longer need, and collapse inactive tab groups for a cleaner tab bar.

4. Customize your workflow
Create custom groups with names, colors, website lists, matching rules, and priorities.

5. Search and recover quickly
Search open tabs, history, bookmarks, and saved snapshots when you need to find something again. Press Cmd/Ctrl+Shift+K to open the Tab Switcher, preview results, and jump to any tab instantly.

6. Manage bookmarks in the same workspace
Search bookmarks, create folders, save the active tab, edit bookmarks, move bookmarks, and delete bookmarks without leaving TabFriday.

7. Use it anywhere
Work from the popup, side panel, or full dashboard depending on how you manage tabs.

TabFriday is built for people who keep many tabs open while working, researching, learning, comparing tools, shopping, or switching between projects.

Less dragging. Less cleanup. Less tab chaos.

Install TabFriday and let your Chrome tabs organize themselves. Free forever.
